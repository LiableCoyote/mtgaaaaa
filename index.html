1 · Your collection

MTG Arena has no public collection API, so you import an export from a third-party tool:

  • MTGA Tool (mtgatool) — tracks your collection live; export it as JSON (a map of Arena card IDs to counts).
  • Untapped.gg companion — exports your collection.
  • Or paste a plain text list like 4 Lightning Strike (one card per line) into the box below — handy for testing or building from a decklist.

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded anywhere; your collection is saved only in this browser's local storage.
